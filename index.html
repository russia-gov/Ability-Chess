Abilities Guide

Captures earn points equal to the captured piece's standard value. Spend those points on one ability before making your normal move.

Shield 3 points

Protect one of your pieces from ordinary capture until your next turn begins. The piece may still move; Bomb is not an ordinary capture.

Freeze 3 points

Choose an enemy non-king piece. It cannot move during its owner’s next turn, but it can still be captured or affected by other abilities.

Disarm 3 points

Click a square carrying an enemy Shield, Freeze, Ambush, Fortify, or Portal effect to remove that effect.

Bomb 4 points

Destroy all pieces in a 3×3 area, including the initiating piece. Neither king may be in the blast, and Fortify blocks the effect.

Swap 4 points

Exchange the squares of two of your non-king pieces. The swap cannot leave your king under attack.

Recall 4 points

Return your most recently moved non-king piece to the square from which it moved, provided the piece is still there and the old square is empty.

Ambush 4 points

Trap one of your non-king pieces. If it is captured before your next turn, the capturing piece is destroyed as well.

Fortify 4 points

Protect a 2×2 area containing your king. Enemy abilities cannot affect your pieces in that area until your next turn.

Teleport 4 points

Move one non-king piece to any empty, unfortified square, then make your normal move. The teleport cannot expose your king or place the teleported piece where it immediately attacks the enemy king.

Reinforce 5 points

Immediately promote one pawn beyond the halfway line to a knight or bishop, then make your normal move.

Portal 5 points

Choose two empty squares. For this turn, a piece entering one portal exits from the other if the exit is empty.

Double Move 6 points

Make two normal chess moves in one turn. A king exposed by the first move can be captured by the second.

Core scoring: pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9. Kings are captured rather than checkmated. You may use at most one ability per turn, and abilities are used before the normal move.
